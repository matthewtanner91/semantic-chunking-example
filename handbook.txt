On-call engineers rotate weekly, starting Monday at 9 a.m. local time. The primary on-call carries the pager and must acknowledge alerts within 15 minutes. A secondary on-call covers escalations if the primary does not respond. Handoffs happen in the team channel with a short summary of open incidents. If you swap shifts, update the rotation calendar before the swap starts. Severity 1 incidents require a written postmortem within five business days. Travel expenses are reimbursed only with an itemized receipt. Economy airfare is the default for flights under six hours. Hotel stays are capped at the city rate listed in the travel policy. Meals are covered up to the daily per diem for the destination. Submit expense reports within 30 days of returning from the trip. Late reports need written approval from your manager. Company laptops must use full-disk encryption at all times. Install operating system security updates within seven days of release. Never store customer data on removable USB drives. Report a lost or stolen laptop to the security team within one hour. Screen locks must activate after five minutes of inactivity. Personal software requires approval before installation on a work machine.
